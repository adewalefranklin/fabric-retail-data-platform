# Business Requirements

The Gold layer was designed around five simulated business requirements.

Management

1. Identify the strongest-performing brands and categories based on price, inventory, ratings and discount levels.

Operations

2. Identify products with low inventory and potential replenishment risk.

Customer Experience

3. Determine which products receive strong, average or weak customer reviews.

Commercial

4. Compare category-level price, stock, rating, discount and product-count KPIs.

BI / Reporting

5. Provide clean product attributes for dashboard filtering and analytical slicing.
